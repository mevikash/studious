Research

Selected research work

My research sits at the intersection of quantum mechanics, computational chemistry, and surface science. Each project is guided by the question: what can atomistic simulation reveal that experiment alone cannot?

Li Isotope Separation via Nuclear Quantum Effects

Current · PhD · UT Austin · Prof. Narayana Aluru

Studying lithium isotope fractionation driven by Nuclear Quantum Effects using Ab Initio Molecular Dynamics, Radial Distribution Functions, Machine Learning potentials, and Path Integral Methods.

AIMDNQEPath IntegralML PotentialsDFT

Electronic-Coupling & Band Gap Tunability in Ferrocenyl Thin Films

Master's Thesis · IIT Kanpur · Prof. T. G. Gopakumar

Developed ultra-thin molecular films with Pd and Cu dopants on HOPG. Formed semiconducting surface-confined metal–organic networks characterised with C-AFM, XPS, and DFT simulations.

AFMC-AFMXPSDFT

SARS-CoV-2 Membrane Fusion Mechanism

UG Project · IIT Kanpur · Prof. Nagma Parveen

Investigated receptor binding and spike protein priming for membrane fusion, with implications for nanoparticle-based therapeutics and structure-guided vaccine design.
